Perseverance

Noun (संज्ञा)
/ˌpɜː.sɪˈvɪə.rəns/
हिंदी अर्थ (Hindi Meaning):
दृढ़ता, लगन, निरंतर प्रयास, अध्यवसाय
English Definition:

Persistence in doing something despite difficulty or delay in achieving success.

उदाहरण (Example Sentence):

"Through hard work and perseverance, she finally achieved her dream."

समानार्थी (Synonyms): Determination Persistence Endurance Tenacity
